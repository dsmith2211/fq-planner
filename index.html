Purchase skills by clicking the Buy button. Skills with code 'M' can be purchased multiple times (capped at 5 unless specified). Skills with code '1' or 'O' can only be purchased once.

Tower Rule: You must have at least 2 spells at level N before purchasing any spell at level N+1. Level 1 has no prerequisite. Your class and school relationship determine cost.

Deities offer special benefits to Clerics and Holy Warriors. Click a deity card to expand details.
